The Rivalry Effect

Quantifying how conflict drives music video views — and what it actually means for long-term growth.

Two East African music videos. Same lead artist (Bien). Dramatically different trajectories. "Finale" hit 10M views in 11 days while "Katam" took 9 months. The obvious answer is "rivalry made it go viral." I wanted the statistical answer.

I decomposed view velocity, classified comment sentiment using basic NLP, and ran a two-proportion Z-test to isolate whether the spike was organic or conflict-driven. It was conflict-driven — and the data proved it.
